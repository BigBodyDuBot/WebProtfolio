Chemistry / Academic project 01

Comparing external calibration and standard addition

Spectrophotometric analysis of potassium ferricyanide using two calibration methods.

UV-VisCalibrationExcelUncertainty

What I did

Prepared calibration standards, measured blank and sample absorbance at 420 nm, and used Excel regressions to compare external-standard calibration with standard addition. Recorded replicate measurements and calculated uncertainty, detection limits and concentrations.

Why it matters

Calibration choice affects how reliably an instrument signal can be translated into concentration, especially when other components in the sample alter that signal.

What came out of it

Produced a full report and calculation workbook documenting the method comparison and a large difference between the two concentration estimates, prompting discussion of matrix effects and measurement uncertainty.

About this coursework
The report records an exploratory method comparison. Agreement with a reference concentration was not established for the unknown; a high calibration R² alone does not establish accuracy.

The report credits Duy Hoang and lab partner Rook Brianna. Original attribution is preserved.
