Data / Academic project 14

Describing data with graphs and summary statistics

Histograms, boxplots and descriptive comparisons across supplied datasets.

StatCrunchHistogramsBoxplotsDescriptive statistics

What I did

Used histograms, boxplots and summary statistics to describe revenue, cholesterol and IQ datasets. Compared medians, variability and group distributions, and discussed possible influences on the observed patterns.

Why it matters

The shape of a distribution affects which summary measures are informative. Graphs also expose spread and group differences that a single average can hide.

What came out of it

Produced a report with graphical summaries and interpretation of center, spread and associations in the assignment datasets.

About this coursework
The comparisons use course datasets and describe associations rather than demonstrating causal effects.
