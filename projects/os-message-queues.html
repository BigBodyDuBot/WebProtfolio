Exchanging messages through a POSIX queue

Separate sender and receiver programs for interprocess communication.

CPOSIXMessage queuesIPC

What I did

Completed C sender and receiver programs that open a named POSIX message queue, exchange messages, handle a termination message and close queue resources.

Why it matters

Message queues let separate processes coordinate through an operating-system-managed communication channel with explicit limits on message size and queue capacity.

What came out of it

Produced two commented source files documenting the send, receive and cleanup paths.

The source preserves the course skeleton comments. The submitted archive’s standalone screenshot is excluded from the portfolio.
