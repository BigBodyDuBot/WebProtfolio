Chemistry / Academic project 01

Measuring concentration with light

External standards and standard addition for ferricyanide analysis.

UV–VisCalibrationExcel

What I did

Prepared a five-point ferricyanide calibration series, collected absorbance measurements at 420 nm, and organized external-standard and standard-addition calculations in Excel.

Why it matters

Comparing calibration approaches helps explain how the sample matrix can influence an analytical measurement.

What came out of it

Produced lab reports, preparation calculations and worksheets that document the measurement process and provide a basis for comparing the two methods.

About this coursework
Analysis draft: calibration formulas and reported fit statistics need reconciliation with the measured data. No validated accuracy or concentration claim is made here.
