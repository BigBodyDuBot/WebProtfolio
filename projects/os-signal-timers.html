Handling periodic signals in C

SIGALRM, signal handlers and a repeating interval timer.

CSIGALRMsetitimerSignals

What I did

Configured sigaction and setitimer to deliver SIGALRM every 250 milliseconds, and used a volatile sig_atomic_t counter to record timer interrupts.

Why it matters

Signal handling exposes how a process responds to asynchronous events and why shared state in a handler needs careful treatment.

What came out of it

Produced commented C source and a short lab write-up describing the timer configuration and interrupt counter.
