Data / Academic project 13

Questioning statistical claims in articles

Written analysis of data sources, context, bias and interpretation.

Statistical literacySource evaluationBias

What I did

Applied a critical statistical literacy framework to education reporting, examining how figures were gathered, what context was omitted, and how different perspectives could change an interpretation.

Why it matters

Evaluating the evidence behind a statistic helps avoid turning an incomplete or selectively framed number into an unsupported conclusion.

What came out of it

Produced a written response that poses questions about sampling, source incentives, confounding factors and comparisons between perspectives.
