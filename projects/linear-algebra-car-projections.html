Projecting a 3D car model with matrices

Toyota Corolla wireframe projections using homogeneous coordinates and Python.

Linear algebraNumPyMatplotlib3D transformations

What I did

Represented the car wireframe with homogeneous coordinates, applied projection, rotation and scaling matrices, and plotted the resulting views with NumPy and Matplotlib.

Why it matters

Matrix operations provide a compact way to connect geometric transformations with computer-generated views of a three-dimensional model.

What came out of it

Produced an illustrated report and plotting source for five transformation cases, connecting the mathematical operations with their visual results.
