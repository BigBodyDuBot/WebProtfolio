Chemistry / Academic project 04

Tracking an indicator’s color change

Spectrophotometric study of bromocresol green across buffer pH values.

SpectrophotometryEquilibriumBuffers

What I did

Collected my own spectrophotometric measurements of bromocresol green in five buffers spanning pH 3.8–5.8 and organized the data for an acid–base equilibrium analysis.

Why it matters

An indicator’s changing absorbance connects a visible color change to the relative amounts of its acid and base forms.

What came out of it

Prepared a lab report and workbook connecting pH-dependent spectra with the method used to estimate an indicator’s pKa.

About this coursework
Analysis draft: wavelength labels, linked workbook calculations and the reported pKa require review. A verified pKa is not claimed.
