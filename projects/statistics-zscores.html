Data / Academic project 17

Using z-scores to compare observations

Standardized observations and the empirical rule in supplied lifestyle data.

Z-scoresEmpirical ruleStandardization

What I did

Explained the empirical rule, calculated z-scores for selected observations, and compared heart rate, height, social-media use, study hours and exercise hours against the supplied distributions.

Why it matters

Standardizing values makes observations measured on different scales easier to compare and highlights values far from their distribution’s mean.

What came out of it

Produced a report with observation and z-score tables. The selected study-hours example was identified as exceeding three standard deviations from its comparison mean.
