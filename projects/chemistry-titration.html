Chemistry / Academic project 03

Standardizing NaOH and analyzing an unknown acid

Primary-standard titration, blank correction and pH-curve analysis.

TitrationKHPpHVolumetric analysis

What I did

Prepared sodium hydroxide, standardized it against potassium hydrogen phthalate with replicate titrations, and recorded pH while titrating an unknown acid. Used Excel to calculate corrected titrant volumes and analyze the titration data.

Why it matters

Standardization establishes the titrant concentration needed for quantitative acid analysis. Replicate measurements and blank corrections help evaluate the reliability of the result.

What came out of it

Produced a full report and workbook documenting a reported NaOH concentration of 0.1066 M, the titration curve and the unknown-acid calculation.

About this coursework
The report contains inconsistent equivalence-volume interpretations. The acid identity and diprotic characterization remain tentative; the portfolio presents the recorded work rather than a confirmed identification.
