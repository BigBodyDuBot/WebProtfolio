Investigating a buffer overflow

A controlled SEED lab on memory safety and defenses.

Memory safetyDockerSEED labs

What I did

Investigated buffer-overflow behavior in a Docker-based teaching environment and documented the roles of address randomization, stack protection and non-executable memory.

Why it matters

Memory-safety failures help explain why defensive controls are needed at both the code and platform levels.

What came out of it

Produced a report explaining the lab behavior and the purpose of the relevant protections.

About this coursework
Controlled academic lab, not a commercial penetration test.
