From 3D coordinates to a 2D view

A Toyota Corolla-inspired wireframe and transformation study.

PythonNumPyComputer graphics

What I did

Represented a model with 16 vertices and an adjacency matrix, then used Python, NumPy and Matplotlib to explore rotations, scaling and perspective projection.

Why it matters

Matrix transformations show how computer graphics translate geometric data into a view on a screen.

What came out of it

Produced named reports documenting five transformation cases and connecting the mathematical operations with the plotted results.
