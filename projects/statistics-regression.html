Data / Academic project 15

Interpreting regression and residual patterns

Linear relationships in Manhattan geometry and Broadway business data.

RegressionR²ResidualsExcel

What I did

Examined a least-squares model for Broadway street geometry, compared business-variable scatterplots, and interpreted residual patterns and R² values for profit and ticket-sales relationships.

Why it matters

A visible trend does not guarantee useful prediction. Residual patterns and explained variance help assess what a fitted model captures.

What came out of it

Produced a regression report with business comparisons reporting R² values of 0.1253 and 0.0548, illustrating the limited predictive value of the examined single-variable models.
