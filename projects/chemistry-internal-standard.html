Chemistry / Academic project 02

Measuring nickel with an internal standard

Nickel and cobalt absorbance ratios used to evaluate concentration and analytical error.

Internal standardAbsorbanceError analysis

What I did

Collected blank and replicate absorbance measurements for nickel and cobalt solutions, calculated corrected signal ratios, and fitted a calibration line to estimate an unknown nickel concentration.

Why it matters

An internal standard helps account for variation in instrument response. Comparing the estimate with a reference value separates measurement agreement from calibration linearity.

What came out of it

Produced a report and Excel workbook documenting the calibration, concentration estimate and sources of error. The report identifies a difference from the instructor-provided reference and proposes improvements in volumetric and cuvette technique.

About this coursework
The original report labels this fluorescence spectroscopy, but its calculations use absorbance. Its stated standard deviation and relative standard deviation require reconciliation before making a precision claim.
