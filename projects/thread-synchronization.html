Software / Academic project 11

Coordinating five concurrent threads

A POSIX threads exercise in shared-resource coordination.

CPOSIX threadsConcurrency

What I did

Used mutexes, condition variables and state tracking to coordinate five threads and compare synchronized behavior with an unsynchronized approach.

Why it matters

Concurrency requires explicit coordination when multiple tasks compete for shared resources.

What came out of it

Created C source and a report that make the synchronization strategy and observed behavior available for inspection.

About this coursework
Coursework demonstration; no formal guarantee of freedom from deadlock or starvation is asserted.
