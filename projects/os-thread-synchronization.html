Coordinating producers, consumers, readers and writers

Semaphore-based thread coordination with recorded terminal output.

pthreadsSemaphoresBounded bufferReaders/writers

What I did

Implemented a bounded-buffer producer/consumer exercise with empty, full and mutex semaphores, and a readers/writers exercise with shared counters and semaphore-protected access.

Why it matters

Coordinating access to shared state helps prevent races and enforces ordering between threads that otherwise run independently.

What came out of it

Produced two C programs and a PDF of lab output showing the producer/consumer and readers/writers exercises.
