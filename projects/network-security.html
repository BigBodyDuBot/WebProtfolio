Observing packets and filtering traffic

Packet-analysis and firewall coursework in an isolated network lab.

ScapyiptablesNetworking

What I did

Used Scapy to explore packet behavior and iptables rules to examine traffic filtering, forwarding and connection tracking in a Docker lab.

Why it matters

Packet inspection and explicit filtering rules help explain how network boundaries permit or deny traffic.

What came out of it

Created reports documenting the lab setup, rule behavior and observations for stateful and protocol-specific traffic controls.

About this coursework
Built on course-provided lab infrastructure. These are academic demonstrations rather than operational network deployments.
