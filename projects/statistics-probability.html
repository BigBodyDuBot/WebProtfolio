Working with conditional probability and Bayes’ theorem

Worked probability calculations using dice and a hypothetical screening example.

ProbabilityConditional probabilityBayes’ theorem

What I did

Counted outcomes for increasing dice sequences, calculated a conditional dice probability, and applied Bayes’ theorem to the assignment’s hypothetical test data.

Why it matters

Conditional probability distinguishes the likelihood of an outcome under a condition from the likelihood of the condition after observing the outcome.

What came out of it

Produced worked solutions, including 5/54 for an increasing three-dice sequence and 1/3 for the conditional dice event, with calculations shown for the Bayes example.
