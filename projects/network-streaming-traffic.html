Software / Academic project 07

Comparing streaming network traffic

A course comparison of traffic volume, packet behavior and streaming stability.

WiresharkHARTraffic analysis

What I did

Compared Netflix, Amazon Prime Video and a third-party streaming service using Wireshark captures and browser HAR observations. Recorded downloaded bytes, packet rates and estimated bitrate, and assembled the findings in a report and presentation.

Why it matters

Traffic measurements can reveal differences in delivery behavior and help separate observed performance from assumptions about a platform.

What came out of it

Produced a joint report and presentation showing the measured traffic comparison and discussion of buffering, encryption and delivery stability in the tested sessions.

About this coursework
The findings describe the tested sessions. Different capture methods and short observation windows limit broader comparisons between services.

The report credits Matthew Polanco and Duy Hoang; collaborator attribution is preserved.
