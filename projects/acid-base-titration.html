Chemistry / Academic project 02

Following an acid–base titration

NaOH standardization and volumetric analysis of an unknown acid.

TitrationStandardizationpH

What I did

Used potassium hydrogen phthalate to standardize NaOH, collected my own titration measurements, and organized pH and volume data into worksheets and a written report.

Why it matters

Standardization connects the amount of titrant delivered to the amount of analyte, making concentration calculations meaningful.

What came out of it

Created an experimental record for examining titration behavior and evaluating the uncertainty in an unknown-acid analysis.

About this coursework
Analysis draft: reported titrant concentrations, equivalence points and stoichiometry are not fully consistent. The files are preserved as coursework, not as a validated identification of the unknown acid.
