Chemistry / Academic project 04

Evaluating repeated measurements of penny masses

Measurement precision, confidence intervals and distribution analysis in Excel.

Measurement statisticsConfidence intervalsGaussian distributionExcel

What I did

Analyzed measured penny masses by mint and year, calculated means, standard deviations and confidence intervals, and compared the distribution with Gaussian expectations. Applied t-tests and a Grubbs outlier test.

Why it matters

Statistical treatment helps distinguish random measurement variation from differences between groups and makes uncertainty in laboratory results explicit.

What came out of it

Produced a report and multi-sheet workbook with histograms, subgroup comparisons, confidence intervals and outlier calculations.

About this coursework
The original report includes conflicting wording in one t-test interpretation. The workbook and original discussion are available for reviewing the calculation and conclusion together.
