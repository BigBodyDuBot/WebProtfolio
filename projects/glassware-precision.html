Chemistry / Academic project 03

How precise is the preparation?

A comparison of volumetric preparation methods using absorbance data.

ReplicatesPrecisionUncertainty

What I did

Collected absorbance measurements for six preparation conditions with five measurements per condition, along with five blank measurements; organized blank correction and variability calculations in Excel.

Why it matters

Replicate measurements help distinguish the consistency of a preparation method from the size of a single reading.

What came out of it

Built a report and calculation workbook for comparing variation across preparation conditions and discussing measurement uncertainty.

About this coursework
Analysis draft: some spreadsheet references and uncertainty calculations require correction before numerical comparisons can be treated as final.
